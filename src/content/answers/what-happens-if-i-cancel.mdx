## The question worth asking any provider

Before buying any automated SEO service, ask one question: *if I cancel, which of these changes
survive?*

With tools that work by injecting changes through a script in your page, the honest answer is
none of them. The title rewrites, the injected schema, the added content — all of it is applied
in the browser by a script that stops loading when the invoice stops. You were renting the
improvements to your own website.

## How this works here

Changes are ordinary commits in your git repository, deployed by your own pipeline. There is no
runtime dependency on us. If you cancel:

- The site keeps working, unchanged, because nothing was ever being injected.
- Every improvement made stays exactly where it is, in your codebase.
- The full history stays too, so any developer can see what was changed and why.
- The changelog remains as a record of the reasoning behind each change.

We stop making new changes. That is the entire effect of cancelling.

## Why we build it this way

Lock-in is a substitute for being worth keeping. A service that can only retain customers by
holding their improvements hostage has told you what it thinks of its own results — and a
provider confident in the work has no reason to build the trapdoor.
