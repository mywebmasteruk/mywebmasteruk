## The question worth asking anyone

Before you buy any service like this, ask one question: *if I cancel, which of these
improvements do I keep?*

With most tools of this kind, the honest answer is none of them. They work by adding a small
script to your website that rewrites your pages while a visitor is looking at them. The moment
you stop paying, the script stops running and your pages quietly go back to exactly how they
were. You were renting improvements to your own website.

## How it works here

The improvements are part of your website itself. There is nothing running on our end holding
them in place. So if you cancel:

- Your site carries on working exactly as it was, because nothing was ever being propped up.
- Every improvement we made stays where it is.
- The full record of what changed and why stays too, so any web person can see the reasoning.
- We simply stop making new changes. That is the entire effect of cancelling.

## And if you would rather have the original back

Before we touch anything, we take a complete copy of your website as it is on day one, and we
keep it for as long as you are a client.

If you ever decide you preferred it the way it was, you can ask for that copy back and we will
put it in place. Almost nobody does — the improvements are the reason people are here — but the
option removes the thing that actually worries people, which is the feeling of handing over
something they cannot get back.

Worth knowing: the copy is your site as it stood on the day we started, so anything you added
yourself since then would need adding again. We will tell you exactly what that is before we
restore anything.

## Why we work this way

Making a customer stay is not the same as being worth staying with. A service that can only
keep people by holding their website hostage has told you what it privately thinks of its own
results.
