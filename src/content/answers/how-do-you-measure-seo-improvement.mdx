## Why before-and-after is not evidence

Traffic moves for reasons that have nothing to do with your work: a core update, a competitor's
site going down, a seasonal swing, a change in how results are displayed. A line that goes up
after an intervention is consistent with the intervention having worked, having done nothing,
or having done harm that was masked by a rising tide.

The whole industry runs on this ambiguity, which is why so much SEO reporting is unfalsifiable.

## The holdout design

Pick pages that are comparable in template, intent and traffic band. Randomly assign each to
treatment or control. Only touch the treatment group. Report the difference in the metric you
committed to before you started.

The cost is real: the control pages do not get the improvements, so you are knowingly leaving
some value on the table to buy a trustworthy number. That trade is worth making, because
without it you cannot tell a working system from an expensive one.

## Choose the window before you look

Search Console finalises data roughly three days after the fact, and a ranking change takes
weeks to settle. A measurement window shorter than that is measuring noise. Fixing the window
in advance — and freezing the page for its duration — is what stops the result being chosen
after the fact to flatter the work.
