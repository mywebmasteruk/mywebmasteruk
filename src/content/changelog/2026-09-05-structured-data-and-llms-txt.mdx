Every page now carries the same clear description of who this business is, rather than several
slightly different ones scattered about. Search engines and AI assistants reading the site see
one business instead of three vaguely similar ones.

Alongside it, there is now a short plain list at a standard address pointing to the pages worth
reading, and every question page is labelled as a question with its answer attached.

None of this creates authority by itself. What it does is remove the confusion that stops
existing content being credited properly, which is a smaller claim and a much easier one to
stand behind.
