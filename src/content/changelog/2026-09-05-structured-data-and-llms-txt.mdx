All structured data is emitted as one `@graph` per page with a single `Organization` node
referenced by `@id`, rather than as disconnected blocks per template. Engines resolving the
graph see one entity instead of several similar ones.

Alongside it, an `llms.txt` at the site root gives answer engines a curated map of the pages
worth reading, and every answer page carries `QAPage` markup with the question and its accepted
answer.

None of this creates authority on its own. It removes the ambiguity that stops existing content
from being attributed correctly, which is a smaller claim and a more defensible one.
