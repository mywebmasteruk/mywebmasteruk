Every measurement from here is compared against this one. It was taken before a line of the new
site went live, straight from Google's own figures, using only settled data so the numbers
cannot quietly change later.

What it describes is a website that Google is perfectly happy to show, and that almost nobody
clicks. Eighteen thousand appearances producing twenty-six visits works out at about one visit
for every seven hundred times we were shown. That is what page four looks like from the inside:
visible to Google, invisible to people.

The thing this site gets shown for most is website maintenance and support. That is a useful
accident, because it is also exactly what Autopilot does.
