Every measurement after this one is relative to this snapshot. It was taken before a single
line of the new site was deployed, from the Search Console domain property, restricted to
final-state data so the numbers cannot shift retroactively.

The picture it describes is a site earning a reasonable volume of search impressions and
converting almost none of them. Eighteen thousand impressions producing twenty-six clicks is a
click-through rate of roughly one in seven hundred, which is what an average position in the
high thirties looks like from the inside: visible to the index, invisible to people.

The most requested thing this domain is shown for is website maintenance and support. That is
a useful accident, because it is also what Autopilot does.
