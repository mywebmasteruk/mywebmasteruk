Fourteen change types, each labelled with the autonomy level it is allowed to reach, whether it
is reversible instantly or by commit, and the signal that triggers it.

Six ship unattended. Six require a human to approve a pull request. Two are permanently out of
bounds: anything touching prices, guarantees, legal text or factual claims about the business,
and anything touching indexing controls such as `robots.txt`, `noindex`, canonicals and hreflang.

The second group is the point. A system that could change what a business charges, or could
deindex it with a single directive, should not exist regardless of how well it performs.
