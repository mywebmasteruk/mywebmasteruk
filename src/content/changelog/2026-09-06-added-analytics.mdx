This site argues that other companies' code is what makes websites slow. Then we added
some. That is worth being straight about.

The reasoning: half of what Autopilot measures is whether visitors actually do anything
once they arrive, and we cannot report that without counting them. So Google Analytics
is now on the site.

What we did to keep it honest. The tag loads only after the page is finished and usable,
so nothing on screen ever waits for it. Nothing is stored on your device unless you agree
— if you decline, we get an anonymous count of the visit and nothing else. Advertising
features are off.

The cost, published rather than buried: the code we send went from 2.2KB to 4.6KB. The
speed figures on the [proof page](/proof/) are measured after this change, not before it.
