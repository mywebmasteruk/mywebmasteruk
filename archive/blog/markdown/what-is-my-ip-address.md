An IP address is a number assigned by your Internet service provider (sort of like a phone number) that determines your geolocation. The results of the IP address lookup will show you the city, state/region, postal/zip code, country name and internet service provider information

To see your IP address go to [whatismyipaddress.com](https://whatismyipaddress.com/?ref=blog.mywebmaster.co.uk)
