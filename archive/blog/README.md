# Blog archive — blog.mywebmaster.co.uk

A complete copy of the retired blog, taken **before** its DNS was repointed.
This existed nowhere else: the `wp2static` exports in Downloads are a different
site, and the `wordpreess` folder held only Divi themes and plugin zips.

- `html/` — every page exactly as served. This is the authoritative backup.
- `markdown/` — readable extraction with front matter, for reuse as content.
- `images/` — every image referenced by a post.
- `index.json` — the manifest: slug, title, original URL, publish date, word count.

22 pages (17 posts, 5 site pages), ~7,950 words, 15 images.

Two posts extract thin because they genuinely are: the website-cost page was an
embedded calculator and the IP page was a lookup widget. Their HTML is saved in
full either way.

Re-run with `python3 fetch.py` while the source is still reachable.

## Why this matters

The post `why-good-website-maintenance-service-is-important` earned **5,605 search
impressions in 90 days** — the single largest signal in the whole dataset, and the
reason the topic is worth rebuilding on the main site rather than simply redirected.
